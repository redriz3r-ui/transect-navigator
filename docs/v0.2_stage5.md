# Stage 5 — batch QA across confirmed Visits

Stage 5 is an additive orchestration layer over the committed Stage 4 review model.
The pre-change QGIS suite passed **367/367 tests**. Existing v0.1 calculation modules,
single-transect UI, schemas, exports and SYN01 data were not changed.

This stage implements:

```text
immutable Stage 3 intake
        +
reviewer-confirmed Stage 4 Visits
        │
        ▼
exact detached Visit input views
        │
        ▼
unchanged v0.1 deviation / statistics / event engines
        │
        ├── independently inspectable Visit QA
        └── observation-level per-transect aggregation and station-support union
```

It does **not** implement automatic GPS-to-transect association, project operational
statuses, a project status map, survey-level completion policy, Stage 6 results, or the
final auditable Survey export.

## Architecture

- `survey/qa.py` contains immutable QA option/result records and pure aggregation.
- `integration/survey_qa_input.py` adapts one exact Stage 4 Visit at a time into the
  established v0.1 ingestion shape without changing the raw archive.
- `integration/survey_qa.py` validates dependencies, runs Visit transactions, retains
  failures, models every registered plan, and aggregates by Transect_ID.
- `integration/survey_qa_storage.py` saves and verifies an internal QA snapshot beside
  a complete Stage 4 review/intake archive. This is resumable analysis state, not the
  future Stage 7 interchange/export package.
- `ui/survey_qa_panel.py` is a small runner and Visit/transect inspector opened from
  the Stage 4 review dialog.
- `tools/survey_qa_fixture.py` independently authors the focused deterministic fixture
  and its numeric oracle. Production QA code is not imported by that generator.

The calculation authority remains the existing `qaqc/deviation.py`,
`qaqc/statistics.py`, `qaqc/events.py`, `geometry/planar.py`, and their tested QGIS
adapters. Stage 5 does not copy their formulas. The original SYN01 Visit is tested
against the direct v0.1 comparison, statistics, events, and independent known answers.

## Eligibility and exact provenance

Only membership satisfying all of these conditions enters a Visit analysis:

- `Assignment_Status = ASSIGNED`;
- `Activity_Role = SURVEY`;
- `Confidence_Class = REVIEWER_CONFIRMED`;
- the effective Decision_ID, Transect_ID, Visit_ID, Recording_ID and Source_ID all
  agree with the current reviewed Visit;
- the normalized working position is valid.

Ambiguous, unassigned and transition observations cannot enter the detached Visit
view. Observations assigned to another Transect_ID cannot enter an aggregation. Failed
or excluded points remain in the authoritative Stage 3/4 archive and disposition
counts; no raw records are deleted.

Each QA sample retains Survey/batch context through its result, Transect_ID and Visit,
Observation_ID, original observation key/source order, Recording_ID, Source_ID,
original and normalized timestamps, nullable analytical timestamp, working WGS84
coordinate, original native geometry WKB, reported accuracy and the v0.1 deviation
record. The nested persisted review archive retains full native attributes, original
source bytes where available, mappings and decision history.

Private planned views are reconstructed from exact official WKB and CRS metadata.
Private observation views copy native geometry/attributes and add a namespaced
Observation_ID. Neither source geometry nor official geometry is modified. The local
analysis CRS and instantiated coordinate operation are recorded per Visit.

## Visit boundaries, chronology and track length

One Visit is one hard calculation group. Source, recording, structural, time/spatial,
reviewed, transition, ambiguous and unassigned boundaries established by Stage 4 are
never crossed. Multiple Visits are never concatenated into a synthetic chronology.

For a Visit with `UTC_SOURCE_ORDER`, Stage 5 builds a private WGS84 line from its
exact ordered points. v0.1 then measures local-AEQD vertex chords within that Visit.
Accepted track length is the sum of known Visit lengths; no edge connects Visits.

For `SOURCE_ORDER_ONLY`, original timestamps remain preserved, but the effective QA
timestamp is NULL and no track is constructed. Point deviation/statistics are still
valid. Time-based events, interval coverage and track length remain unevaluated. If
any accepted Visit has unknown track length, the aggregate `Recorded_Length_m` is
NULL; `known_track_length_m` and `unknown_track_visit_count` disclose the known
subtotal and missing portion instead of reporting a misleading zero or partial total.

A failed Visit is recorded with exact identity/error and no partial QA sample output.
Other Visits continue. Transect summaries disclose accepted versus evaluated counts,
failure messages, and partial/failed processing flags.

## Direction, partial and repeated Visits

Official planned direction always defines station and signed deviation: positive is
left and negative is right relative to official digitization. A reverse Visit keeps
the same plan; stations ordinarily decrease over time. Absolute deviation and coverage
remain invariant for equivalent forward/reverse paths. The fixture verifies that a
north-side reverse walk still has positive sign on an east-directed official line.

Partial Visits are legitimate. Positional adherence is summarized for the accepted
observations, while support length/percent reports only the observed interval. A clean
0–60 m Visit can therefore have near-zero deviation and 60% support without being
labelled a failed survey. Completion classification is deferred to an explicit Stage 6
policy.

Repeated Visits remain separate, auditable results. Their observations all contribute
to the explicitly observation-weighted deviation/GNSS descriptions. Per-transect
means, medians, RMSE, percentiles and within-threshold percentages are recomputed from
the underlying accepted observations; Visit summaries are never averaged.

## Support and event aggregation

Two distinct support measures are retained:

- **Observed progress support** reuses the v0.1 interval-union algorithm with a private
  zero-deviation support view. It retains the existing chronology, station-gap and
  time-gap rules but does not apply the QA corridor.
- **QA corridor coverage** is the unchanged v0.1 support rule and additionally requires
  both adjacent deviations to be within user threshold C.

Both union intervals by station across independent Visit_ID groups. Repeated coverage
cannot exceed planned length. Complementary fixture Visits spanning 0–60 m and
50–100 m produce 100 m union support, not 110 m and not an arithmetic mean, while
accepted track length correctly remains 110 m.

Events are detected independently per Visit with the unchanged v0.1 rules. The familiar
display ID (`T01_E001`) may repeat across Visits, so Stage 5 adds a deterministic
globally unique `event_uid` derived from dependency and Visit identity. Exact event
Observation_ID membership and Visit provenance are retained. Moving event geometry is
stored in the Visit analysis CRS; stationary events retain SQL/typed NULL geometry
semantics rather than an empty line. A gap, reviewed split, transition or unresolved
interval cannot be bridged by one event.

GNSS summaries remain separate, retain source units, and never correct/subtract spatial
deviation. The UI labels 3/5/10 m as editable QA thresholds, not regulatory or
scientific compliance standards.

## Dependency identity and persistence

A QA dependency fingerprint covers:

- the Stage 4 `qa_input_digest`, including Survey/plan/intake identity, exact effective
  dispositions, Visit membership and review revision;
- complete QA options;
- a SHA-256 content map of calculation, CRS, adapter and identity modules;
- QGIS/PROJ version context and configured coordinate operations.

UI names, selection and cosmetic styling are excluded. Changing plan/source identity,
field/time mappings, surveyor identity, assignment or Visit membership, QA options,
engine code or transform context rejects a saved/current result as stale. A stale
result can remain visible as historical data in the inspector but cannot be saved as
current and is never silently reused. Running QA creates a fresh result; Stage 5 has
no hidden cache.

`save_batch_qa()` stages a new no-overwrite directory and verifies a full round trip:

```text
reviewed_qa/
  README.txt
  qa.sqlite
  review/
    review.sqlite
    intake/
      survey_intake.gpkg
      survey_intake_manifest.json
      sources/...
```

`qa.sqlite` contains the complete typed analysis run plus relational Visit, transect,
observation and event rows. Restore verifies its checksum/tables, decodes only declared
result types, revalidates current dependencies, reopens the archived review/intake,
and verifies exact Visit/sample/event links. Existing package names are refused without
modification. A moved whole package remains readable.

## Results UI

The Stage 4 review dialog now includes **RUN / INSPECT VISIT QA…**. The panel exposes
editable statistics/coverage/event options, runs the current review, and shows:

- current/stale dependency state and Visit/transect failures;
- a transect table with Visit and accepted/evaluated counts, planned/track lengths,
  mean/median/RMSE/P95/maximum deviation, progress/corridor support, events and flags;
- a Visit table retaining direction, state, count, track/deviation/support/events;
- JSON detail for exact statistics, GNSS description, event membership and provenance;
- verified save/reopen actions.

The UI does not assign observations, infer completion or alter QGIS project layers.
Changing review decisions immediately marks displayed QA stale.

## Independent fixture and numeric oracle

`samples/survey_qa/` has two east-directed 100 m equatorial lines and three continuous,
unlabelled CSV recordings (40 observations). Independently authored review truth is:

- 35 assigned, 2 ambiguous, 3 unassigned including 2 transitions;
- 4 Visits: full forward T01 with a sustained event, full reverse T02, partial forward
  T01 (0–60 m), and partial reverse T01 (100–50 m);
- T01 aggregation: 24 observations, 3 Visits, mean 1.75 m, median 0 m,
  RMSE `sqrt(438/24) = 4.2720018727 m`, P95/maximum 12 m,
  within 3/5/10 m = 87.5%, recorded length 220.62404485 m, progress/corridor 100 m;
- T02: 11 observations, mean `9/11 = 0.8181818 m`, median/P95/maximum 1 m,
  RMSE `sqrt(9/11) = 0.9045340 m`, recorded length 100.09975124 m,
  progress/corridor 100 m;
- one T01 event: 3 observations, 20 seconds, 20 m station span, mean/maximum 12 m;
- removing the full T01 Visit leaves complementary partials with 13 observations,
  110 m accepted track and 100 m unioned progress/corridor support;
- the full event Visit has 100 m progress but only 60 m QA corridor support.

The generator uses direct equatorial WGS84 construction and closed-form arithmetic;
it imports no production Transect Navigator module. Numeric tolerance is 0.02 m for
the local projection/reference approximation and is not widened from observed output.
Tests also cover mixed native CRS, unknown chronology, stationary/moving events,
actual 5 km model failure isolation, source/plan/assignment/options/engine invalidation,
storage-order invariance, persistence/tampering/no-overwrite, Qt runner inspection, and
exact SYN01 v0.1 equivalence. SYN01 files remain unchanged.

## Validation and limitations

Focused test command under configured QGIS Python:

```shell
python3 -m unittest tests.test_survey_qa tests.qgis.test_survey_qa
```

The automated UI tests use QGIS offscreen mode. They verify controls, tables, stale
state and the Stage 4 WKB/map regression, but do not constitute interactive GUI
validation. Manually test window sizing, real mouse/keyboard selection, long-running
feedback and large-survey responsiveness in QGIS.

Verified on 2026-10-07 with QGIS 4.2.3's bundled Python 3.12 on macOS:

- recovered pre-change Stage 4 baseline: **367 passed**, 14.530 seconds;
- recovered Stage 5 focused checkpoint: **28 passed**, 8.335 seconds;
- final focused suite after the invalid-WKB regression: **29 passed**,
  6.335 seconds;
- final post-documentation complete suite: **396 passed, 0 failed, 0 errors,
  0 skipped**, 18.881 seconds, process exit code 0;
- `python3 -m compileall -q transect_navigator tests tools` and
  `git diff --check` passed.

Offscreen Qt printed nonfatal platform messages about size hints/window raising and a
font-alias fallback. No crash, test failure, unexplained skip or teardown error occurred.
No release metadata, ZIP, v0.1 QA engine or Stage 6 component changed.

Stage 5 execution is synchronous and keeps detached per-Visit results in memory.
Cancellation/progress, large-dataset performance bounds, automatic association,
long-line tiling, project statuses/map, survey-level lower-bound coverage, source/person
overview, completed Survey export and concurrent editing remain deferred. Unknown
chronology intentionally prevents track/coverage/event claims even when an analyst can
defensibly accept points for deviation QA.

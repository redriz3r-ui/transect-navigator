## Components and result model

- `survey/results.py`: immutable policy, append-only relevance review, per-plan
  results, status precedence and length-weighted summary. No QGIS dependency.
- `integration/survey_results.py`: current-batch validation, dependency identity
  and a detached native-CRS status layer.
- `integration/survey_results_storage.py`: internal verified SQLite results snapshot
  with nested existing QA/review/intake persistence. This is not final Survey export.
- `ui/survey_results_panel.py`: explicit configuration, summary/table, map, filters,
  unresolved evidence scoping, Visit/QA/event detail, save/open.
- `ui/survey_qa_panel.py`: additive SURVEY RESULTS action and validity notification.
- `tools/survey_results_fixture.py`, `samples/survey_results/`, and two new test
  modules: independent oracle, real QGIS integration and pure operational rules.

Every official Transect_ID has exactly one result, including unsupported plans and
plans with no accepted Visits. Duplicate/missing/foreign result rows are rejected.
Each result retains the complete unchanged Stage 5 `TransectQa`, with Visit IDs,
accepted/evaluated counts, native model length, track length, progress/corridor support,
QA statistics, GNSS statistics, events, errors and source/recording/surveyor provenance.
Added fields include status/basis, policy identity/reviewer, endpoint evidence,
relevant unresolved Observation_IDs, current QA state, and confirmed support.
The Survey result records plan/review/batch/dependency identities and creation UTC.

## Explicit completion policy and precedence

`CompletionPolicy` requires an ID, version (`progress-and-endpoints-1`), required
support percentage, endpoint allowance in metres, numerical tolerance, selected-by,
reason and UTC. The UI provides suggested controls but nothing is selected until an
analyst supplies identity/reason and clicks **SELECT THIS OPERATIONAL POLICY**.
The numerical allowance is restricted to 0–0.1 m; it is not a walking-deviation limit.
Policy content, not just its name, enters dependency identity and saved provenance.

Completion requires unioned, gap-limited observed progress at or above the selected
percentage (allowing the explicit numerical tolerance), plus support near both ends.
Endpoint support requires a nonzero accepted station interval reaching the configured
endpoint allowance. The existing Stage 5/v0.1 support engine validates those endpoint
pairs. A singleton endpoint, rejected gap, or chronology-unknown Visit is not endpoint
support. Official direction stays authoritative; a reverse Visit works identically.
Deviation thresholds, GNSS quality and event counts do not decide completion.

Precedence is deterministic:

1. **REVIEW** for unsupported/failed processing, relevant unresolved evidence, or
   accepted Visits with unknown chronology/track or excluded support groups. These
   cannot be hidden behind another full Visit. Stale whole snapshots are separately
   blocked from current display/use and shown as STALE / REVIEW.
2. **NOT_SURVEYED** when there are no accepted Visits and no preceding review reason.
   This means no accepted work in currently imported data, not intentionally skipped.
3. **COMPLETE** when accepted evidence satisfies the selected progress/endpoints policy.
4. **INCOMPLETE** when accepted work exists but that policy is unmet.

Suggested_Status and Final_Status currently agree: Stage 6 has no manual completion
override or reported-skip operation. Reviewer is the recorded policy selector; this
does not assert a separate human judgment of every final line status. Statuses are
**operational workflow classifications, not regulatory/scientific determinations**.

## Unresolved evidence without automatic assignment

Stage 4 deliberately stores AMBIGUOUS/UNASSIGNED observations without candidate plan
IDs. Stage 6 therefore cannot infer their relevance to a line. Until explicitly scoped,
every unresolved non-transition observation conservatively requires REVIEW on every
plan. The UI lets an analyst select exact unresolved rows and all relevant plans,
recording reviewer/reason/UTC and the assignment fingerprint. This only records
relevance: it creates no Visit, accepted assignment, or QA observation.

An empty plan selection explicitly records unrelated UNASSIGNED evidence. AMBIGUOUS
evidence cannot be dismissed this way. Transitions are already excluded by Stage 4.
Relevance revisions append; later entries supersede earlier scoping for their exact
members. History remains intact. On a changed assignment fingerprint old scopes are
retained as historical, become ineffective, and require explicit re-review. There is
no automatic migration, nearest-line assignment, or hidden exclusion.

Survey unresolved counts are unique observations, including explicitly unrelated
unassigned points. Per-line relevant counts can overlap and must not be summed into
the Survey count. Shared ambiguity can legitimately require REVIEW on multiple plans
without giving those plans accepted support. All raw and assignment data stay intact.

## Aggregation and unknowns

Use Stage 5 local-model planned length consistently; PREPARE ellipsoidal length remains
separate inside QA records. For modeled line lengths `L`, confirmed union progress `P`
and confirmed union QA corridor `C`:

```text
confirmed progress lower bound (%) = 100 * sum(P) / sum(L)
confirmed QA corridor lower bound (%) = 100 * sum(C) / sum(L)
accepted track = sum(known within-Visit measured track lengths)
```

Line support is already unioned by Stage 5. Repeated Visits never receive duplicate
station credit; support exceeding planned length is rejected (tiny floating-point
roundoff is clamped). Track length can exceed planned length. No means or percentiles
are averaged and no per-point QA is recalculated. The summary contains all four status
counts, planned/modeled/unknown counts, distances, support, source/surveyor counts,
unresolved/unscoped/ambiguous/unassigned counts and processing state.

Unobserved/unresolved intervals contribute no *confirmed* support. Underlying unknown
evaluated measurements remain NULL in the retained QA record. Chronology-unknown or
failed accepted work adds unknown-support counts; accepted-track total is NULL if any
Visit track is unknown, with a known subtotal alongside it. Unsupported plan lengths
make exact total planned length and whole-survey percentages NULL; the known subtotal
and missing-plan count are disclosed. The denominator is never silently reduced.
Counts reconcile to all plans. Processing is PARTIAL for missing lengths/unknown
support/errors, FINISHED_WITH_REVIEW for unresolved evidence/review lines, otherwise
FINISHED. This describes the reviewed QA snapshot; the separate Stage 3 import ledger
remains authoritative for failed/partial/duplicate import attempts.

## Map and RESULTS workflow

From the existing Survey review dialog choose **RUN / INSPECT VISIT QA…**, run QA,
then **SURVEY RESULTS…**. The installed v0.1 single-transect menu path is unchanged.

1. In **Policy / unresolved evidence**, enter policy ID, reviewer, reason and explicit
   support/endpoint/numerical settings; select the policy.
2. Select exact unresolved observations and all relevant plan IDs; record relevance
   with a reason. Leaving uncertainty unscoped is permitted and conservatively visible.
3. **BUILD / REFRESH SURVEY RESULTS** validates current dependencies and builds totals.
4. Filter by status, Transect_ID, accepted surveyor (including UNKNOWN), or unresolved/
   REVIEW. Filters do not change evidence or call QA. Status map retains all plans.
5. Select a line to highlight/zoom its geometry and inspect existing QA summaries,
   Visits, events, exact provenance and relevance history. The existing Stage 5 Visit
   inspector is updated as well.
6. **SAVE RESULTS SNAPSHOT…** to a new folder; **OPEN RESULTS SNAPSHOT…** restores it
   against the same current Survey/review/QA settings.

The panel owns one read-only memory status layer and map canvas. Each feature copies
official native WKB and CRS, including NULL geometry for unsupported/null plans.
There is no source styling/data change, project layer insertion, or layer proliferation.
Fields include identity/status/basis, lengths, Visit/observation counts, progress,
corridor, unresolved count, QA state, policy, source and surveyor IDs. Categorized
styles are blue COMPLETE, ochre INCOMPLETE, purple REVIEW, grey NOT_SURVEYED. Labels
explicitly say operational. The map is a snapshot with dependency metadata, not an
independent live analytical authority.

## Staleness and persistence

Dependencies include the complete Stage 5 batch content/fingerprint (which already
binds plan, raw intake, review, QA parameters, engine and QGIS/PROJ transform context),
full selected policy, relevance history, and Stage 6 rule code hashes. Review/QA-option
changes notify an open RESULTS panel. Validation also occurs when reopening the panel,
building, saving or loading. External data is validated at these boundaries rather
than polled continuously.

A stale snapshot cannot be saved or treated as current. Current totals/table/map are
cleared; the historical immutable result is retained internally. No mixed current/stale
totals appear. Rerun Stage 5 QA when its inputs changed. Policy or scoping changes only
require Stage 6 recomputation. Re-select edited policy controls explicitly.

Internal persistence uses standard files:

```text
new_results_snapshot/
  README.txt
  results.sqlite       # typed results + policy + relevance history + checksum
  qa/                  # existing verified Stage 5 QA/review/native-source archive
```

The existing exclusive-publication primitive refuses existing names. Save stages and
reopens everything before publication. Load verifies checksum, full underlying QA/
review/raw archive, then reproduces operational aggregation from saved QA and policy;
even a modified summary with a matching rewritten checksum fails reproduction. No
per-observation geometry QA is run on load. This is resumable internal state, not the
Stage 7 GeoPackage/CSV Survey export or adversarially signed evidence.

## Remaining limits / Stage 7 boundary

- Real interactive GUI validation is still required: window sizing, map colors/zoom,
  mouse/keyboard range selection, file dialogs, and close/reopen workflows. Tests use
  real QGIS/Qt offscreen and do not claim manual acceptance.
- Relevance is human supplied; unscoped evidence may conservatively put many lines in
  REVIEW. No automatic proposals or inference of intent.
- No manual completion override, explicit skipped disposition, or policy migration.
- Source/person details are retained in Visit drill-down and raw archives; surveyor
  filtering refers to accepted Visits, not inferred crew identity.
- No standalone survey-menu redesign, status-layer publication to the main project,
  live cross-window external-source polling, concurrency, or automatic review migration.
- Stage 7 must design the auditable interchange export; internal results persistence
  must not be misrepresented as that export. No implementation blocker is known from
  this focused fixture, but field scale and real GUI acceptance remain unverified.

## Independent construction and truth

[`samples/survey_full`](../samples/survey_full/README.md) contains a declared
10 × 10 km site, twelve official 1 km WGS84 geodesic plans, a projected EPSG:32631
companion, three externally supplied surveyor identities and five original returned
sources: A1.csv (293), B1.gpx (208), B2.gpkg (101), C1.geojson (217), C2.csv (189).
No returned file contains Transect_ID labels. `tools/survey_full_fixture.py` uses an
independent WGS84 Vincenty direct construction and writes `expected.json`, including
explicit one-based review ranges. It calls no production Transect Navigator engine
to generate coordinates, decisions or expected statistics. Repeated generation was
verified byte-for-byte identical, including both GeoPackages after freezing GDAL's
wall-clock `gpkg_contents.last_change` field.

The intended source ranges are: A1 T01 (101), transition (15), T02 reverse (101),
transition (15), T03 0–600 m (61); B1 T04 0–400 plus 500–1000 (92), transition (15),
T05/T06 ambiguity (101); B2 T07 full (101); C1 T07 reverse (101), transition (15),
T08 full (101); C2 T10 400–1000 (61), transition (15), T11/T12 ambiguity (101),
unrelated (12). An analyst's independently authored decisions, not nearest-line
logic, produce 719 ASSIGNED, 202 AMBIGUOUS and 87 UNASSIGNED fixes, of which 75
are TRANSITION. All 1,008 Observation_IDs have one disposition. T04's 500 s / 100 m
gap creates two Visits. The 12 unrelated C2 CSV fixes form a separate **continuity**
segment via a large spatial/time break; the current CSV parser has no explicit
multiple structural-recording-segment input. This is a documented representation
limit, not a dropped observation.

## Full Stage 2–7 exercise

The QGIS integration test uses `setup_survey`/`prepare_survey_navigation`, exports
the standard survey navigation package, imports all five original files, checks
Source_ID/Recording_ID/Observation_ID provenance and exact source counts, then
replays `expected.json` decisions through `ReviewRun.decide`. It saves and reopens
review; computes Visit and per-transect QA with unchanged v0.1 cores; applies an
explicit operational completion policy and relevance scopes; verifies status map;
exports the reviewed Survey with the current navigation snapshot; and calls the
standalone QGIS GeoPackage verifier. It checks exported source SHA-256, all 1,008
assignment memberships, T07 repeated-Visit provenance, T08 statistics/event,
uncertain lines and no-overwrite. Exact and renamed B2.gpkg duplicate import attempts
are audited but leave five accepted sources, five recordings, 1,008 observations,
Visits, support and statuses unchanged.

Isolated variants additionally verify crossing-line ambiguity; missing/duplicate
timestamps, clock reversal and unknown accuracy; stops and an unknown surveyor;
overlapping nonidentical files retained with warning; invalid GPX and NULL point
geometry retained as failures/unassignable observations; SQL NULL stationary event
and nonstationary LineString; review revision staling old QA and successful reanalysis;
storage/import order invariance; projected plan companion; and reversal of official
geometry flipping signed error while preserving absolute error/support. A separate
12 m excursion variant remains operationally COMPLETE from full progress while its
QA-corridor support decreases. None of these variants alters the main oracle.

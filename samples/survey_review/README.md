# Focused Stage 4 review fixture

These files and the decision oracle were authored directly, not generated by the
Transect Navigator engine. This is deliberately a focused review fixture, not the
later large Stage 8 survey. Both plans are parallel east-directed WGS84 lines from
longitude 0 to 0.001, at latitudes 0 and 0.001. It is sufficient to know positions,
membership and direction; Stage 4 does not calculate deviations or coverage.

| Source | Surveyor | Recordings | Observations | Scenario |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A.csv | S01 | 1 | 14 | T01 forward; transition; T02 reverse; ambiguous middle; unrelated point; partial T01 repeat after GPS/time gap |
| B.gpx | S02 | 2 | 6 | Two structural segments on T01; separate reverse T02 recording |
| C.csv | unknown/NULL | 1 | 4 | Missing and duplicate time; ambiguous fixes; unrelated point |

CSV maps x/y in EPSG:4326, time and accuracy; GPX maps time and HDOP. The files have
no authoritative Transect_ID field. `note` is human-readable fixture explanation,
not an input used by the association engine. `expected.json` supplies independently
authored analyst decisions by source/track and 1-based observation row (excluding
CSV header). Tests resolve those rows to persistent exact Observation_IDs before
review. Production state never stores these row ranges as authoritative membership.

With explicit gaps of >60 seconds or >100 metres, expect 10 segments and 24 retained
observations. Apply the oracle actions: 15 ASSIGNED, 5 AMBIGUOUS, 4 UNASSIGNED,
including 2 TRANSITION and 2 unrelated. Seven observations remain unresolved; 6 Visits
are accepted (4 FORWARD, 2 REVERSE). C's missing/duplicate times never gain chronology.
B's two structural segments never join even though their gap is small. A's partial
repeat does not join earlier work. Source files, IDs and original geometry remain
unchanged after decisions, edits and save/reopen.

From the repository, with configured QGIS Python:

```shell
python3 -m unittest tests.qgis.test_survey_review -v
```

Tests create disposable review SQLite/intake GeoPackage archives, verify actual
membership/provenance and reopen them. No generated package is committed. See
`docs/v0.2_stage4.md` for workspace use and limitations. SYN01 remains unchanged.
